How to set this up

About 10 minutes, one time
  1. Choose when you are free. Below, tick the days you take appointments, then set your hours, how long each visit lasts, and how much notice you need. Check that the time zone is yours.
  2. Say how you will meet. Write it the way a client would read it, like "Phone call. I will call you at the number you give me." If it mentions a phone or a call, clients will be asked for their number.
  3. Connect your own calendar. Under "Hide times when I am already busy," paste the link to the calendar on your phone. Anything already on it, like showings or closings, is hidden from clients so you are never double booked. Tap the kind of calendar you use and follow the steps for just that one.
  4. Block your days off. Add holidays, vacations, or any day you are not working under "Days off." Those days will not show to clients.
  5. Turn it on. Tick "Turn on my booking page" and tap Save. Your link to share appears right under the box.
  6. Try it yourself. Tap "See what clients see," pick a time, and book with an email you can check. You will get the client's confirmation and your own "new booking" note. Then cancel it under "Coming up."
  7. Put it on your phone. Open this page on your phone, signed in, and tap "Add to my iPhone calendar" (then Subscribe), or "Add to Google Calendar" on Android. Bookings, transaction deadlines and pie pickups will all show up there. Phones check for changes about once an hour, so new bookings can take a little while to appear.
  8. Share your link. Tap "Copy link" and paste it into texts, emails, your email signature, or your social profiles.

When someone books, you both get an email, they get a friendly reminder the day before, and it shows under "Coming up." You can cancel any booking there and they will get a kind note.

Where do you keep your appointments? Tap one to see the steps.

Let's find out together.
  1. On your iPhone, open the Calendar app.
  2. Tap Calendars at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Find the calendar your appointments go on. Look at the small gray word above it.
  4. It says GMAIL or GOOGLE? Tap Google Calendar above.
  5. It says ICLOUD? Tap iPhone (iCloud) above.
  6. It says OUTLOOK or EXCHANGE? Tap Outlook above.
  7. It says ON MY IPHONE? That calendar only lives on your phone, so a website cannot see it. Ask us for help moving it to iCloud.

Have an Android phone? Your calendar is almost always Google. Tap Google Calendar above.

Google Calendar (you need a computer for this part)
  1. On a computer, go to calendar.google.com and sign in.
  2. Click the gear at the top right. Then click Settings.
  3. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click your name.
  4. Under your name, click Integrate calendar.
  5. Find Secret address in iCal format. Click the copy button next to it (it looks like two little squares).
  6. Come back to this page. Click the box above that says Paste a calendar link here.
  7. Paste it (Ctrl+V on a PC, Cmd+V on a Mac). Then click Save.
  8. You will see a green box that says Connected. You are done!

Please do not click Reset in Google. That would break the link and you would need to do this again.

iPhone (iCloud)
  1. On your iPhone, open the Calendar app.
  2. Tap Calendars at the bottom.
  3. Under ICLOUD, find the calendar your appointments go on. Tap the little i next to it.
  4. Scroll all the way down. Turn on Public Calendar.
  5. Tap Share Link, then tap Copy.
  6. Open this page on your phone. Tap the box above that says Paste a calendar link here.
  7. Tap the box again and tap Paste. Then tap Save.
  8. You will see a green box that says Connected. You are done!

"Public" just means someone would need this exact link to see it. The link is long and random, and we keep it private. Just do not post it anywhere. Do not see Public Calendar? Then this calendar is not iCloud. Tap I am not sure above.

Outlook (you need a computer for this part)
  1. On a computer, go to outlook.com (or outlook.office.com for a work account) and sign in.
  2. Click the calendar icon on the left.
  3. Click the gear at the top right.
  4. Click Calendar, then Shared calendars.
  5. Find Publish a calendar. Pick your calendar. Pick Can view when I'm busy. Click Publish.
  6. Two links appear. Click the ICS link and choose Copy link.
  7. Come back to this page. Paste it in the box above that says Paste a calendar link here. Then click Save.
  8. You will see a green box that says Connected. You are done!

No Publish a calendar section? Some work accounts turn it off. Ask your office tech person to allow calendar publishing.

Good to know: changes on your calendar show up here within a few minutes. All-day events, like birthdays or holidays, do not block times. Use Days off for those. Use more than one calendar? Add each link the same way.
