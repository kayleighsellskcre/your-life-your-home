How to set this up

About 10 minutes, one time
  1. Choose when you are free. Below, tick the days you take appointments, then set your hours, how long each visit lasts, and how much notice you need. Check that the time zone is yours.
  2. Say how you will meet. Write it the way a client would read it, like "Phone call. I will call you at the number you give me." If it mentions a phone or a call, clients will be asked for their number.
  3. Connect your own calendar. Under "Hide times when I am already busy," paste the link to the calendar on your phone. Anything already on it, like showings or closings, is hidden from clients so you are never double booked. Tap "How to get the link" for the steps.
  4. Block your days off. Add holidays, vacations, or any day you are not working under "Days off." Those days will not show to clients.
  5. Turn it on. Tick "Turn on my booking page" and tap Save. Your link to share appears right under the box.
  6. Try it yourself. Tap "See what clients see," pick a time, and book with an email you can check. You will get the client's confirmation and your own "new booking" note. Then cancel it under "Coming up."
  7. Put it on your phone. Open this page on your phone, signed in, and tap "Add to my iPhone calendar" (then Subscribe), or "Add to Google Calendar" on Android. Bookings, transaction deadlines and pie pickups will all show up there. Phones check for changes about once an hour, so new bookings can take a little while to appear.
  8. Share your link. Tap "Copy link" and paste it into texts, emails, your email signature, or your social profiles.

When someone books, you both get an email, they get a friendly reminder the day before, and it shows under "Coming up." You can cancel any booking there and they will get a kind note.

How to get the link
iPhone Calendar
  1. Open the Calendar app and tap Calendars at the bottom.
  2. Tap the i next to the calendar you put your appointments on (under iCloud).
  3. Turn on Public Calendar, then tap Share Link and choose Copy.
  4. Paste it in the box above and tap Save. Use more than one calendar? Add each one the same way.

"Public" only means anyone who has this exact link could see that calendar. The link is long and impossible to guess, and it is kept private here, so just do not post it anywhere. If your calendars are not under iCloud (for example they are under Gmail), use the Google steps instead.

Google Calendar
  1. On a computer, open calendar.google.com.
  2. Click the gear, then Settings, then your calendar's name on the left.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the Secret address in iCal format.
  4. Paste it in the box above and tap Save.

Changes on your calendar show up here within a few minutes. All-day events, like birthdays or holidays, do not block times. Use Days off for those.
