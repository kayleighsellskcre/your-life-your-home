How do I update my home's estimated value? ▾
Go to Value & Equity from the top nav and click any of the snapshot cards. Each one has an edit button that lets you update the numbers anytime.
How do I add or manage multiple properties? ▾
If you have multiple properties linked to your account, a property selector will appear at the top of the Value & Equity page. You can switch between them there.
Who can see my information? ▾
Only you, and the agent or lender connected to your account. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.
How do I reach my agent or lender directly? ▾
Go to the Contact Us tab above and click "Chat with a Human." You can also use the Ask a Question form and they'll respond to you directly.
Can I upload my own home documents? ▾
Yes. Head to Care → Documents & Timeline in the top navigation. You can upload closing docs, warranties, receipts, and more.
What's the difference between this platform and my agent just texting me? ▾
This platform gives you a permanent home for all your property data, documents, equity tracking, renovation plans, and more, so nothing gets lost in a text thread. Your agent can still text you, but now you also have everything organized in one place.
