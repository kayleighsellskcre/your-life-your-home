Save money, stay comfortable

Energy Savings

Start with the easy wins, then plan the bigger upgrades. Most of this pays you back in lower bills and a more comfortable home.

Easy wins this weekend

  • Swap in LED bulbs in the rooms you use most.
  • Change your HVAC filter every one to three months. A clogged filter makes the system work harder.
  • Seal the gaps. Weatherstripping and caulk around doors, windows and outlets on exterior walls stop drafts.
  • Turn your water heater to 120 degrees. It's safer and uses less energy.
  • Use a programmable or smart thermostat to set back the temperature while you're asleep or away.

Bigger upgrades, in order of payback

  • Attic insulation and air sealing. Usually the biggest comfort and bill improvement for the money.
  • A home energy audit. Many utilities offer one free or low cost, and it shows you where your home loses the most energy.
  • Efficient heating and cooling. A modern heat pump or high-efficiency system pays off over time, especially when yours is near the end of its life.
  • Windows and doors. Worth it when they're damaged or very old. Seal and shade first, since replacement takes longer to pay back.

Find rebates and incentives

Rebates change often and depend on where you live and what you buy, so check the source before you buy.

  • ENERGY STAR Rebate Finder, by zip code and product.
  • DSIRE, a database of state and local incentives.
  • Your electric or gas utility. Many offer rebates and audits. Check their website or call.
  • Your tax professional. Federal energy tax credits have changed recently, so confirm what applies to you this year.
