Getting Started with Calendly

What is Calendly?

Calendly is the leading scheduling platform that eliminates the back-and-forth of finding a meeting time. Share your calendar link and let borrowers book directly into your availability.

Setup in 3 Steps

  1. 1

    Create a Calendly Account

    Visit calendly.com and create a free account. You can sign up with your email, Google, or Outlook account.

  2. 2

    Configure Your Availability

    Set your working hours, timezone, and meeting types. Create different meeting types for loan consultations, pre-approvals, and follow-ups. Connect your calendar to prevent double-bookings.

  3. 3

    Share Your Link

    Get your unique Calendly link and share it via email, text, your website, or agent partners. Borrowers will see your availability and book directly.

Embed on Your Website

Once you have your Calendly account set up, you can embed your calendar directly into your lender profile page or website using this code:

<div class="calendly-inline-widget" data-url="https://calendly.com/your-username" style="min-width:320px;height:630px;"></div>
<script type="text/javascript" src="https://assets.calendly.com/assets/external/widget.js" async></script>

How to customize:

Replace "your-username" with your actual Calendly username. You can adjust the height and width to fit your page layout. For more customization options, check the Calendly documentation.
