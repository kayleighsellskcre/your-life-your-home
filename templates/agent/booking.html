Connect Your Calendar in One Step

You are one minute away from a fully live booking page.

Add your Calendly link (or any scheduling link like Acuity, MS Bookings, or Google Booking) in your Settings. Once saved, a live interactive calendar appears right here for clients to book from.

Go to Settings to Add Your Link

Not on Calendly yet? Here is how to get started:

  1. 1

    Create a Free Calendly Account

    Go to calendly.com and sign up with your Google or Outlook account. Takes about 2 minutes.

  2. 2

    Connect Your Calendar and Set Your Hours

    Connect Google or Outlook so Calendly reads your real availability. Set the days and times you want to accept bookings. Create meeting types like "Buyer Consultation" or "Listing Appointment."

  3. 3

    Paste Your Link into Settings

    Copy your Calendly link (it looks like calendly.com/yourname) and paste it into the Schedule URL field in your Profile Settings. Save, and your calendar goes live here instantly.

Pro tip: Add your booking link everywhere

Once your Calendly is live, copy the link and add it to your email signature, your website contact page, your text templates, and your social media bio. Every time someone clicks it, they land on a page that does the scheduling for you automatically, no back and forth.
